Fertilizer 🌾 Rice Published
Rulepriority 20 · Vegetative · DELAY_FERTILIZER

Nitrogen topdressing at the vegetative stage

Apply urea in two splits — the first at 14–21 days after transplanting, the second before panicle initiation. Avoid application when heavy rain is forecast to prevent nutrient loss.

📖 PhilRice · Rice Production Manual · 2024
Updated Aug 10, 2026

Irrigation 🌾 Rice Published
Rulepriority 30 · Reproductive · SKIP_IRRIGATION

Water management through the reproductive stage

Maintain a shallow water level of 2–5 cm during panicle initiation and flowering. Drain fully 7 days before harvest to firm the field for machinery.

📖 IRRI · Water-Saving Guidelines · 2023
Updated Aug 08, 2026
03
Irrigation 🍅 Tomato Published

Preventing blossom-end rot with steady irrigation

Blossom-end rot follows irregular watering and low calcium uptake. Mulch to hold soil moisture and keep irrigation even, especially through flowering.

📖 DA-ATI · Tomato Production Guide · 2024
Updated Aug 05, 2026
04
Fertilizer 🍅 Tomato Published
Rulepriority 20 · Fruiting · DELAY_FERTILIZER

Potassium side-dressing at the fruiting stage

Apply a potassium side-dressing at the onset of fruiting to improve fruit size and quality. Water it in lightly and avoid topdressing during heavy rain.

📖 DA-BAR · Tomato Best Practices · 2023
Updated Jul 29, 2026

Planting 🌽 Corn Published

Planting density and spacing for hybrid corn

Space seeds 75 cm between rows and 20–25 cm within rows to reach the target population of 50,000–55,000 plants per hectare at harvest.

📖 PhilMaize · Hybrid Corn Manual · 2024
Updated Aug 02, 2026

Harvest 🧅 Onion Published

Harvest timing and bulb curing

Harvest when 60–70% of leaves have lodged. Cure bulbs in the shade for 2–3 weeks before topping and storing to extend shelf life.

Harvesting and de-handing banana bunches

Harvest when the fingers are three-quarters full and angular. De-hand the bunch carefully and transport gently to avoid bruising and hasten ripening.
